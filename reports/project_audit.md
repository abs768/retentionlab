## Leakage Controls

- **Identifier exclusion.** `customerID` is dropped in `data.split_features_target` before any
  model sees the data, filtered again defensively in `features.infer_feature_types`, and
  asserted absent from the fitted transformer's `feature_names_in_` by a test.
- **Train-only preprocessing.** Every imputer, scaler, and encoder lives inside a
  `Pipeline`/`ColumnTransformer` fitted on the training split alone. A test asserts the
  numeric imputer's learned medians equal the training-split medians. The only pre-split
  operations are stateless (numeric coercion, target mapping).
- **Untouched test set.** The test split is scored exactly once, in `pipeline.evaluate_on_test`,
  after both the model choice (validation PR-AUC) and the calibration choice (validation
  Brier) are final. No test-set quantity feeds any selection decision.
- **Split integrity.** A test asserts the three splits are pairwise disjoint, jointly cover the
  dataset, hold prevalence to within one point of the overall rate, and are reproducible under
  a fixed seed while changing under a different one.

Strongest measured claims, all traceable to `reports/`:

- 7,043 customers, 19 model features, 26.5% churn prevalence; 0 duplicate identifiers and 11
  unparseable `TotalCharges` values, all belonging to zero-tenure customers.
- Model selected on validation PR-AUC (0.643 gradient boosting vs 0.628 logistic regression),
  not accuracy — the ordering differs by metric, and F1 would have chosen differently.
- Final held-out test performance, scored once: ROC-AUC 0.855, PR-AUC 0.670 against a 26.5%
  prevalence baseline, Brier 0.1324, log loss 0.4058.
- Sigmoid calibration improved the validation Brier score from 0.13919 to 0.13809 — a real but
  small gain (0.8% relative), accompanied by a slight PR-AUC decrease from 0.643 to 0.639.
- Risk-ranked outreach at 20% capacity reaches 51.1% of test-set churners (143 of 280) at
  67.5% precision among those contacted — 2.55x random targeting. Top 10%: 28.6% capture,
  2.85x. Top 30%: 66.1% capture, 2.20x.
- A randomized retention trial on that high-risk population (67.5% observed baseline churn)
  would need 3,915 customers per arm to detect a 3-point absolute reduction at 80% power, or
  5,241 at 90%; a 1,000-per-arm pilot detects nothing smaller than about 6 points.

## Limitations

- **Public sample dataset.** IBM's fictional Telco Customer Churn sample, not real customer
  records. Metric magnitudes should not be read as benchmarks for an actual telco.
- **Observational churn labels.** The data records outcomes only; it contains no retention
  action, offer, or contact.
- **No treatment assignment.** None exists in the data and none was fabricated.
- **No causal retention claim.** Policy lift measures ranking quality on historical outcomes,
  not the effect of contacting anyone. The experiment-design module sizes the trial that could
  answer the causal question; it does not answer it.
- **Modest dataset size.** A single 1,057-row test split; differences of a point or two are
  within noise, and no confidence intervals are reported.
- **Not a production deployment.** No serving layer, monitoring, retraining schedule, or
  temporal validation. Hyperparameters are fixed rather than searched, and the policy counts
  churners rather than dollars.
